Save for later

This whole app is one HTML file. Save it once and it keeps working without this site, without internet and without installing anything.

  1. Hit Download below. You get a file called recorder.html.
  2. Keep it anywhere: Desktop, Documents, a USB stick.
  3. Double-click it. It opens in your browser and works exactly like this page.

Chrome, Edge and Safari record MP4. Firefox records WebM. Recordings download straight to your computer and are never uploaded. Offline, the page uses your system font instead of Nunito.
